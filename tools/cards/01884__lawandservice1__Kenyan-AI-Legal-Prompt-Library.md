# Kenyan-AI-Legal-Prompt-Library
Welcome to the Kenyan AI Legal Prompt Library.

This project aims to make Kenyan legal knowledge freely accessible using Artificial Intelligence.

## Features

- 📚 Legal Research Prompts
- ⚖️ Civil Procedure
- 🏛 Constitutional Law
- 👨🏾‍⚖️ Criminal Law
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Law
- 📜 Succession Law
- 🌍 Land Law
- 🏢 Company Law
- 📄 Court Document Templates
- 📝 Contract Drafting
- 📖 Study Notes
- 🎓 Law Student Resources
- 🤖 AI Prompt Engineering for Lawyers

## Who is this for?

- Law Students
- Advocates
- Judicial Officers
- Researchers
- Legal Clerks
- Court Process Servers
- Members of the Public

## Mission

To make Kenyan legal education and legal drafting more accessible through responsible use of Artificial Intelligence.

## Disclaimer

This repository is for educational and research purposes only. It is not legal advice. Always verify authorities and consult a qualified Advocate of the High Court of Kenya before relying on AI-generated legal work.
