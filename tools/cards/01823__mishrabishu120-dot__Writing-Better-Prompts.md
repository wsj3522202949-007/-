# SkillCraft Technology AI Internship

## Task 01 – Writing Better Prompts

### Overview

This repository contains my submission for **Task 01 – Writing Better Prompts** completed as part of the **SkillCraft Technology Artificial Intelligence Internship Programme**.

The objective of this task was to understand the fundamentals of Prompt Engineering by transforming vague prompts into structured, detailed, and effective prompts while analyzing how prompt quality affects AI-generated responses.

- Reflection and Conclusion
