A dual-signal web application that classifies text as **AI-generated** or **human-written** using a RoBERTa-based classifier combined with GPT-2 perplexity scoring — two independent signals that together produce more reliable detection than either approach alone.

---

## Overview

As large language models become increasingly capable, distinguishing AI-generated content from human writing is a growing challenge across content moderation, academic integrity, journalism verification, and regulatory compliance.

Single-model approaches have known weaknesses — classifiers can be fooled by paraphrasing, and perplexity alone varies too widely across domains to be reliable. This project combines both signals into a **heuristic scoring framework** that is more robust than either in isolation.

### Use Cases

| Domain | Application |
|---|---|
| **Content Moderation** | Detecting AI-generated spam, synthetic reviews, bot content |
| **Academic Integrity** | Identifying AI-assisted submissions |
| **AI Output Auditing** | Verifying whether content was human or machine authored |
| **Journalism** | Flagging potentially synthetic news content |
| **Legal & Compliance** | Documenting provenance of AI-generated vs. human-authored materials |

---

## Features

- **Dual-signal detection** — RoBERTa classifier confidence + GPT-2 perplexity scored independently then combined
- **Combined AI-likelihood score** — weighted heuristic combining both signals for improved reliability
- **Transparent outputs** — all three scores displayed individually so users understand each signal's contribution
- **Clean Streamlit UI** — interactive, browser-based, no setup required for end users
- **CPU and GPU compatible** — runs locally without requiring GPU

---

## How It Works

```
Input Text
    ├──→ RoBERTa Classifier (roberta-base-openai-detector)
    │         ↓
    │    Classifier Confidence Score
    │    (probability text is AI-generated, 0–1)
    │
    └──→ GPT-2 Language Model
              ↓
         Perplexity Score
         (how "surprising" the text is to a language model)
         Low perplexity → likely AI-generated
         High perplexity → likely human-written

Both signals
    ↓
Heuristic Combination
    ↓
Combined AI-Likelihood Score + Visual Output
```

### Why Two Signals?

**RoBERTa classifier** (`roberta-base-openai-detector`) was fine-tuned specifically to detect GPT-2 output. It is fast and accurate on text similar to its training distribution but can be fooled by paraphrasing or text from models it wasn't trained on.

**GPT-2 perplexity** measures how predictable the text is to a language model. AI-generated text tends to have lower perplexity (more predictable token sequences) than human writing. This signal is model-agnostic — it doesn't depend on the specific LLM used to generate the text.

**Combined heuristic** — when both signals agree, confidence is high. When they disagree, the combined score reflects the uncertainty, which is itself informative.

## Example Outputs

| Input | Classifier Score | Perplexity | Combined Score | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI-generated paragraph | 0.94 | 18.3 | 0.91 | 🤖 AI-generated |
| Human-written essay | 0.12 | 187.4 | 0.09 | ✍️ Human-written |
| Paraphrased AI text | 0.61 | 42.1 | 0.58 | ⚠️ Uncertain |

> Note: Perplexity varies by domain and text length. Short texts and highly technical content may produce less reliable scores.

## Limitations

- RoBERTa classifier was fine-tuned on GPT-2 output — performance may vary on text from newer models (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini)
- Short texts (<50 words) produce less reliable perplexity scores
- Highly technical or domain-specific text may exhibit naturally low perplexity, affecting classification
- This tool is intended as a detection aid, not a definitive classifier — human judgment remains essential in high-stakes decisions
