# Keystone Writer
Keystone Writer is a powerful desktop writing app for novelists and authors. Outline plots, manage characters in a dynamic story bible, write your manuscript, visualize timelines, and export professionally. The all-in-one tool for crafting your next masterpiece. Built with Electron &amp; React.
