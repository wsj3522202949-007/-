# HAND-SIGN-DETECTOR
Hand Sign Detector AI is a real-time computer vision system that recognizes hand gestures and converts them into text and speech. It uses hand-tracking to detect finger landmarks, classify gestures, and supports custom sign training, helping in sign language learning, communication support, and interactive human-computer interaction.
