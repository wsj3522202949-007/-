## What is Storyist?

**Storyist** is a comprehensive writing application tailored for authors, screenwriters, and creative professionals who require a structured yet flexible environment for long-form writing. It combines the simplicity of a word processor with the organizational power of a project management tool, allowing writers to focus on what matters most: their story.

The software offers a borderless, full-screen writing mode that minimizes distractions and maximizes productivity. Writers can easily navigate between chapters, manage character profiles, and visualize plot lines through built-in storyboarding tools. Storyist also supports industry-standard formatting for novels and screenplays, making it a versatile choice for both fiction and non-fiction writers.

With seamless iCloud integration, your work is automatically synced across all your Apple devices, enabling you to write anywhere, anytime. Whether you are drafting a novel, composing a screenplay, or outlining a series, Storyist provides the tools you need to bring your ideas to life.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Visit the official Storyist website or App Store.
2. Download and install Storyist on your device.
3. Open the app and select a template that fits your project.
4. Begin writing using the borderless focus mode.
5. Use the project sidebar to manage story elements.
6. Sync your work via iCloud to continue on other devices.
