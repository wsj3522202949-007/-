# Superdom AI 🤖

**No-Code Agentic AI Platform for Enterprises**

Superdom AI empowers businesses to build and deploy intelligent AI agents without writing code. From customer support copilots to automated marketing workflows, we make enterprise AI accessible to everyone.

## What We Build

- 🤝 **AI Copilots** - Intelligent assistants that augment your team's capabilities
- 🚀 **AI Autopilots** - Fully automated workflows that run independently
- 🔄 **Multi-Agent Systems** - Coordinated AI teams that collaborate to solve complex tasks
- 🧠 **RAG-Powered Solutions** - Knowledge-grounded AI with enterprise data integration

## Built For

- Small and medium businesses looking to scale with AI
- Marketing and sales teams automating outreach and content
- Enterprise operations streamlining support and processes
- Teams who want AI power without engineering complexity

## Technology

Powered by cutting-edge frameworks including LangChain, CrewAI, and Model Context Protocol (MCP), with enterprise-grade security and scalability.

## Open Standards We Support 🌐

Our founder contributes to the open-source AI community through **[agentpolicy.org](https://agentpolicy.org)** - an open standard protocol that defines how websites can control AI agent access and interaction with their content. Think of it as "robots.txt for the agentic web."

🔗 [View the Agent Policy Protocol Specification](https://github.com/agent-policy-protocol/spec)

As AI agents become more prevalent, standards like this ensure responsible and controlled agent behavior across the web, enabling website owners to define clear policies for how AI systems can interact with their content.
