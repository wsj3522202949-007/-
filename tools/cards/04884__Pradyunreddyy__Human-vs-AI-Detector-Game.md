# Human-vs-AI-Detector-Game
Challenge your ability to distinguish human-written text from AI-generated content in this interactive quiz. Read each prompt, choose whether it was written by a human or AI, get instant feedback, track your progress across rounds, and sharpen your understanding of modern AI writing patterns.

A few things about how it works:

•20 curated rounds across four types — text, code, a voice clip (transcript + a "play" button using your browser's speech synthesis), and small generative SVG artworks — each written to have a genuine tell (a leftover debug print, a self-correction mid-sentence, perfect radial symmetry vs. a wobbly hand-drawn line, etc.). Each playthrough samples 10 at random.
•Answering reveals a rotated "stamp" (like a court document) showing the true origin, plus a short explanation of the tell.
Score and streak are tracked live in the header, with a streak toast every 3 in a row.
•At the end, you can save your name and score to a shared leaderboard that persists across sessions and is visible to anyone who opens the file (I flagged that in the UI itself).
