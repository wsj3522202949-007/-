# 🎬 Narrate-O-Vision

An AI-powered cinematic storytelling system that automates the entire movie creation pipeline — from script generation to visual rendering, voice narration, and final video production.

This project integrates deep learning and generative AI to transform a simple text prompt into a complete cinematic experience.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Narrate-O-Vision is a multi-stage AI system that converts user prompts into structured movie-like outputs. It combines Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, and Audio Synthesis to create immersive storytelling.

The system follows a fully automated pipeline:
1. AI Story Generation  
2. AI Image & Video Generation  
3. AI Voice Narration  
4. Final Cinematic Video Creation  

---

## ⚙️ Workflow

### 1️⃣ AI Story Generation
- Uses a Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) with LSTM layers  
- Trained on structured movie script datasets  
- Generates screenplay-style output including:
  - Characters  
  - Dialogues  
  - Emotions  
  - Scene descriptions  

---

### 2️⃣ AI Image & Video Generation
- Stable Diffusion generates cinematic images from scene descriptions  
- Scene-based visuals are created automatically  
- MoviePy applies animations such as:
  - Zoom effects  
  - Fade transitions  

---

### 3️⃣ AI Voice Narration
- ElevenLabs generates realistic human-like voiceovers  
- Different tones and voices for characters  
- Synchronizes narration with visuals  

---

### 4️⃣ Final Video Generation
- MoviePy merges:
  - AI-generated images  
  - Voice narration  
- Produces final cinematic video output (MP4 format)
