# 📱 Social Media Content Automation | CSQ-Based GPT Workflow (AI + CRM)

## 🔍 Project Overview
Automated the creation of platform-specific social media content for enrolled agents using a GPT-powered prompt system. This end-to-end workflow was triggered by agent-submitted Content Survey Questionnaires (CSQs) and synchronized across Dynamics 365 CRM, enabling rapid and brand-aligned content generation without manual copywriting.

## 🧠 Business Problem
Content creation for social media was time-intensive, requiring writers to interpret agent responses manually and tailor posts for each social platform (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.). As order volumes scaled, this approach led to content delays, inconsistent tone, and bottlenecks in the delivery pipeline.

## 🎯 AI-Powered Solution
Agents enrolling in a digital marketing program via a third-party tool triggered downstream workflows:
- Enrollment data was synced to CRM as an order.
- Internal teams populated writer, editor, and content live/end dates.
- Using agent-submitted CSQs, a GPT-based tool automatically generated social media copy tailored to each platform.
- Content creation and feedback workflows were handled within CMS, enabling writers and editors to review and finalize posts.
- AI prompts were continuously refined based on engagement feedback and brand voice requirements.

## 🤖 AI Relevance
- **Prompt Engineering**: Designed custom GPT prompts to turn CSQ responses into channel-optimized, brand-aligned posts.
- **Tone Tuning**: Adjusted content tone and formatting per platform
- **Scalability**: Enabled AI to replace manual writing, improving delivery speed and reducing dependency on creative bandwidth.

## 🔑 Key Features
- **CSQ-to-Post Translation**: Automated generation of social media copy from structured questionnaire data.
- **Platform-Specific Prompts**: GPT outputs were customized by channel type (FB, IG, LI).
- **AI Prompt Refinement**: Feedback loop with AI engineers to improve content quality and relevance.
- **Brand Voice Alignment**: Ensured GPT responses respected tone guidelines for consistency.
- **Documentation & Training**: Created internal guides and trained team members on AI-generated content workflows.

## 👩‍💼 Business Analyst & AI Consultant Responsibilities
- Led the design and refinement of GPT prompt logic based on CSQ data inputs.
- Mapped workflows from agent enrollment to content publishing using process diagrams.
- Collaborated with AI engineers to adjust outputs per engagement trends and internal feedback.
- Defined business rules for tone, length, hashtags, emojis, and platform-specific formatting.
- Documented prompt architecture and usage expectations for future training and scaling.
- Provided ongoing support and feedback during rollout to internal content teams.

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM
- Internal GPT-based AI Tool
- Internal Content Management System (CMS)
- Microsoft Visio (Process Flows)
- Microsoft Teams & Outlook
- SharePoint (Training Materials)

## 📊 Key Outcomes
- Reduced social content turnaround
- Eliminated dependency on manual copywriting for templated content
- Improved content consistency across thousands of agent accounts
- Enabled scalable AI workflow to meet growing digital marketing demand
