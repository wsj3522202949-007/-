# AI-Powered-PM-Toolkit
8-week mentorship project for 15–20 IIT Kanpur students covering PM fundamentals (RCA, CIRCLES, RICE, MoSCoW), LLM mechanics, prompt engineering, and AI-assisted PRD writing — culminating in a capstone e-commerce sprint where mentees diagnose real pain points and ship a custom AI bot or automation workflow as final deliverable.
