# 🌍 Cross-Cultural Project Management Dashboard

A React-based web tool designed to support global teams by providing **culturally sensitive insights** on communication, scheduling, feedback, decision-making, and holiday planning — with AI-powered assistants for writing, scheduling, and risk assessment.

This app integrates established cultural frameworks such as:
- **Hofstede’s Cultural Dimensions**
- **Erin Meyer’s Culture Map**
- **Trompenaars’ Seven Dimensions of Culture**

Built with project managers, global teams, and multicultural stakeholders in mind.

---

## 🧠 What This Project Does

- 📊 Compare multiple countries on communication styles, feedback norms, scheduling expectations, and decision-making models  
- 🧠 Generate **AI-powered risk analyses** for team frictions across cultures  
- 💌 Get **email writing suggestions** tailored to cross-cultural tone and hierarchy  
- ⏰ Find **time zone overlap** and culturally respectful scheduling slots  
- 📅 Visualize national holidays for planning around peak disruptions  

All insights are grounded in real business practices and academic research — not stereotypes — and designed to be **actionable, respectful, and realistic**.

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters

Cross-cultural misunderstandings are one of the top reasons global projects fail — often not due to technical issues, but misaligned expectations, communication breakdowns, or unintentional offense.

As someone managing multinational teams in Japan, I built this tool to:
- Improve **global collaboration**
- Enhance **cultural awareness**
- Reduce **decision delays and meeting conflicts**
- Support smoother communication across time zones and norms

---

## 🚀 Features

| Category | Feature |
|----------|---------|
| 🌐 **Country Selector** | Choose 1–7 countries to compare across cultural traits |
| 🧠 **Communication & Feedback Insights** | Based on academic models, with actionable advice |
| 🧠 **AI Risk Analyzer** | Identifies top 3 cross-cultural risks + mitigation strategies |
| 💌 **AI Email Assistant** | Recommends phrasing and tone based on selected cultures |
| 🕰️ **Overlap Visualizer** | Graphical view of work-hour overlaps (in JST reference) |
| 📆 **Holiday Calendar** | Consolidated list of major business-disrupting holidays |
| ✨ **Smart Scheduler** | Suggests meeting times across time zones with cultural notes |
| 🇬🇧/🇯🇵 **Bilingual Ready (planned)** | Designed for future expansion into Japanese interface |
