## ✦ What is PromptLift?

PromptLift is a free, browser-based AI writing toolkit built for everyone — students, freelancers, creators, and small business owners — who struggle with writing or prompting AI tools effectively.

No login. No signup. No installation. Just open and use.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Inside

| Tool | What it does |
|------|-------------|
| **Prompt Improver** | Turns weak ideas into powerful AI prompts for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini |
| **Bio Writer** | Writes professional bios for LinkedIn, Upwork, Instagram, Fiverr |
| **Caption Generator** | Creates engaging captions with hashtags — supports image upload |
| **Email Writer** | Writes professional emails in any tone and length |
| **Subject Line Generator** | Creates high-converting email subject lines |
| **Recents** | Saves your last 20 generations locally on your device |

---

## ⚡ Features

- 100% free to use
- Works on all devices — mobile, tablet, desktop
- Image upload for caption generation
- Recent generations saved locally (localStorage)
- Copy to clipboard on every output
- Regenerate button for fresh results
- No backend, no database — pure HTML/CSS/JS
- Fast loading even on slow connections

---

## 🧰 Tech Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3 (custom, no frameworks)
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Claude AI API (Anthropic) for generation
- localStorage for recents
- Google Fonts (Syne + DM Sans)
