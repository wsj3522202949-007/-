# 📊 AI Prompts for Pitch Deck Writing

Welcome to **AI Prompts for Pitch Deck Writing** — a powerful guide to generate compelling, professional, and investor-ready pitch decks using the power of AI.

Whether you're a startup founder, entrepreneur, or marketer, this guide provides you with ready-to-use prompts that work with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any AI model to craft perfect slides — from problem to solution, market size to financial projections.

---

## 🚀 Why Use AI for Pitch Deck Writing?

Creating a pitch deck can be time-consuming and overwhelming. With AI, you can:

- ✍️ Craft persuasive slide content tailored to investors
- 🔍 Validate your business idea with structured narratives
- ⚡ Speed up deck creation by 70%+
- 📊 Customize tone, format, and slide count with ease
- 🎯 Receive instant feedback and iterations

---

## 🧠 How to Structure Your AI Prompts

To get effective results, include the following in your prompts:

- **Startup Name & Industry**
- **Business Model** (e.g. B2B SaaS, DTC product)
- **Target Audience** and **Problem**
- **USP / Innovation**
- **Funding Stage** (Pre-seed, Seed, Series A)
- **Tone** (Professional, Visionary, Minimalist, etc.)

---

## ✅ 10 Prompt Examples with Outputs

---

### 1. 📌 Problem Slide

**Prompt:**  
> Write a "Problem" slide for a B2B SaaS platform helping HR teams reduce hiring time.

**Output:**  
> **Problem:**  
> - Hiring top talent takes too long (avg. 42 days).  
> - HR teams lack centralized tools for screening, scheduling, and feedback.  
> - Inefficient workflows cost companies top candidates and increase turnover.

---

### 2. 💡 Solution Slide

**Prompt:**  
> Create a "Solution" slide for a mobile health tracking app for diabetic patients.

**Output:**  
> **Solution:**  
> GlucoTrack is an AI-powered app that helps diabetic patients monitor blood sugar, receive personalized diet alerts, and connect with care providers — all in real time.

---

### 3. 🧩 Product Demo

**Prompt:**  
> Write a product demo slide for a marketplace that connects freelance chefs with events.

**Output:**  
> **Live Product Demo:**  
> - Event planners browse verified chefs  
> - Book instantly by date, cuisine, or budget  
> - In-app messaging & seamless payments

---

### 4. 🌍 Market Opportunity

**Prompt:**  
> Create a market size slide for a sustainable pet food brand targeting US millennials.

**Output:**  
> **Market Opportunity:**  
> - $48B U.S. Pet Food Market  
> - 68% of millennial pet owners prefer sustainable products  
> - Our TAM: $12B | SAM: $2.5B | SOM: $300M

---

### 5. 🔥 Unique Value Proposition

**Prompt:**  
> Write a UVP slide for an AI tool that summarizes legal documents.

**Output:**  
> **Why Us:**  
> - AI-trained on 10M+ legal docs  
> - Saves lawyers 8+ hours/week  
> - Integrates with Microsoft Word & Google Docs

---

### 6. 💰 Business Model

**Prompt:**  
> Write a business model slide for a subscription-based language learning app.

**Output:**  
> **Business Model:**  
> - Freemium app with $9.99/month Pro plan  
> - Annual subscriptions: $99/year  
> - Add-on revenue: live tutoring, branded certificates

---

### 7. 📈 Traction Slide

**Prompt:**  
> Create a traction slide for a startup that launched 6 months ago with 10K users.

**Output:**  
> **Traction:**  
> - 10K users in 6 months (40% MoM growth)  
> - $20K MRR  
> - Featured in TechCrunch & Product Hunt  
> - 300+ paying teams

---

### 8. 👥 Team Slide

**Prompt:**  
> Write a team slide for a fintech startup founded by two ex-bankers and a CTO from Stripe.

**Output:**  
> **Team:**  
> - Jane Kim (CEO): Ex-Goldman Sachs, 10Y in lending  
> - Alex Wong (COO): Ex-Citi, ops leader  
> - Priya Ramesh (CTO): Built fraud systems at Stripe

---

### 9. 💸 Financial Projections

**Prompt:**  
> Generate a 3-year revenue projection slide for a DTC coffee subscription startup.

**Output:**  
> **Financial Forecast:**  
> - Year 1: $450K  
> - Year 2: $1.2M  
> - Year 3: $3.4M  
> - CAC: $24 | LTV: $110 | Gross Margin: 62%

---

### 10. 📢 Ask / Use of Funds

**Prompt:**  
> Write the "Ask" slide for a startup raising a $1M seed round.

**Output:**  
> **We’re Raising $1M Seed**  
> - 50% Product development  
> - 30% Sales & Marketing  
> - 10% Legal & Ops  
> - 10% Hiring
