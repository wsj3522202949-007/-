## 🚀 How to Use

1. Browse the `prompts/` folder.  
2. Copy & paste prompts into your favorite AI tool.  
3. Replace placeholders (`{topic}`, `{text}`, `{code}`) with your own content.  
4. Experiment and share improvements!
